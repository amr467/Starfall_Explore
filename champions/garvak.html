Garvak believes that strength alone proves worthiness — “Stand firm. Break worlds.” He measures himself against the world’s other elemental powers without hesitation: “Lava bends. Stone endures,” he tells Rhaegor; “You burn hot. I burn forever,” he tells Teostra; and to Thorall, “Your strength is borrowed. Mine is built.”

Condemned

Serathiel sees none of this as righteousness. To her, Garvak is a war-god worshipper who has judged himself worthy without ever earning it. When Garvak raised his war-cannon toward a village, Serathiel’s golden seals closed around him before he could fire. “I have judged myself worthy,” he laughed. “Then dawn will disagree,” she answered — and a single beam pierced the battlefield.

“Judgment is earned, not claimed,” Serathiel has told him since.

A dent in his plating, oddly mace-shaped, predates any record of Bogrund’s arrival in the Machine Wastes.

Abilities (Overview)

  • Passive — The Siegeborn: Standing still grants stacking Siege Charges, increasing attack range, ability damage, and resistances; moving removes stacks.
  • Q — Hellbore Shot: A massive empowered shot from his back cannon, growing in area with Siege Charges.
  • W — Anchoring Plates: Roots Garvak in place, instantly maxing his Siege Charges, reducing damage taken, and granting nearby allies bonus resistances.
  • E — Shatter Pulse: A ground slam that knocks back and, while anchored, slows enemies.
  • R — Earthbreaker Round: Consumes all Siege Charges to fire a massive piercing shot that leaves a burning, slowing trail.
